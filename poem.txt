In Conway's grid of black and white,
Cells awaken, fade from sight.
Born with three, survive with two,
Simple rules make patterns new.

Gliders sail across the plane,
Blinkers pulse in their refrain.
From chaos springs a structured dance,
Life emerges—not by chance.

Each generation, fate unfolds,
Stories that the grid beholds.
In this game of death and birth,
Complexity finds its worth.
