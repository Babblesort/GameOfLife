The Game of Life

In a grid of black and white,
Where simple rules ignite,
A universe unfolds its dance,
Born from mathematics' glance.

Each cell awaits its fate,
Counting neighbors at the gate,
Three to birth, two or three to stay,
All others fade away.

Gliders sail across the plane,
Blinkers pulse in their refrain,
Still lifes rest in perfect peace,
While chaos finds release.

From random seeds, patterns grow,
Emergent beauty starts to show,
Complex forms from simple laws,
Life without a cause.

Conway's vision, pure and bright,
Proves that order springs from slight
Rules that govern, cell by cell,
Stories only time can tell.

In this digital domain,
Life and death in one campaign,
A reminder, small but true:
Simple rules make worlds anew.
