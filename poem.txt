Life as a Game

Each day a board where we must play,
With pieces moving, come what may,
The rules are written, yet unclear,
We navigate through joy and fear.

Some moves bring triumph, some bring loss,
We calculate the hidden cost,
Yet in the game, we find our way,
Learning more with every day.

The game continues, round by round,
In patterns strange, yet patterns found,
Life's greatest gift, we come to see,
Is playing with uncertainty.
